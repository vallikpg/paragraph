Instructions for Students

Write a single, well-developed paragraph for each chosen topic.

Your paragraph must include:

  1. A clear Topic Sentence that introduces the main idea.
  2. At least 3 Supporting Sentences with vivid details, facts, or examples.
  3. Appropriate Transition Words (e.g., furthermore, in addition, however, for instance, as a result, finally) to connect your ideas smoothly.
  4. A strong Concluding Sentence that restates the main idea or summarizes your thoughts.
  5. Proper indentation, punctuation, capitalization, and spelling.

Aim for around 60 words per paragraph. Paragraphs under 50 words will be penalized.
